## Standard

The site is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at
Level AA, which includes every WCAG 2.1 Level AA criterion.

## What we have done

- Semantic HTML with landmarks, headings in order and a skip link to the main content.
- Text and interface contrast checked against WCAG ratios, including text over images and video.
- Every function available from the keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
- Pages that reflow to a 320-pixel-wide screen and survive enlarged text spacing without loss of content.
- Motion that respects your device's reduced-motion setting{{PAUSE_SENTENCE}}.
- Descriptions for meaningful images; decorative images hidden from assistive technology.
- No accessibility overlay or widget; the site itself is built to be accessible.

## How we tested

Automated testing with axe-core against WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules
at desktop, mobile and 320-pixel widths; automated focus-visibility,
reflow and text-spacing checks; and review of every page in its normal and
reduced-motion states. {{MANUAL_TESTING_SENTENCE}}
